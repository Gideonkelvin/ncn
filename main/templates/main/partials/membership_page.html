Growing in Faith, Together

Become part of our vibrant and welcoming church community. Membership is more than a formality—it is a meaningful expression of your commitment to grow in Christ alongside others. It provides an opportunity to build deeper relationships, be rooted in sound discipleship, and actively participate in the life and mission of the church.

As a member, you are invited to use your gifts and passions to serve and make an impact. There are several ministry areas where you can get involved:

  • Ushering Ministry – Creating a warm and orderly worship environment
  • Teens Church – Guiding and nurturing the next generation
  • Media Team – Supporting services through technology and communication
  • Women’s Ministry – Encouraging fellowship and spiritual growth among women
  • Children’s Ministry – Teaching and caring for young hearts in faith
  • Praise and Worship Team – Leading the congregation in heartfelt worship

Membership also includes the opportunity to have your photograph included in our church family directory and related member materials, where applicable.
