About Us

Founder and Purpose

Rose Coloured Lens NPC was founded to advance visual impairment awareness and visual inclusion in South Africa. Our founder brings lived experience and professional expertise in low vision advocacy and corporate visual health strategy.

We believe that visibility, clarity, and evidence-based messaging are essential for lasting change in how organisations and communities approach visual health and inclusion.

Our Vision

A South Africa where visual health is prioritised, low vision is understood and supported, and visual inclusion is embedded in corporate and community practice.

Our Mission

What We Commit To

We deliver on our vision through focused, credible programmes and partnerships. Our mission is reflected in everything we do.

  • Advance visual impairment awareness and low vision advocacy through education and storytelling.
  • Support visual inclusion in South Africa via keynote speaking and corporate visual health strategy.
  • Build trusted resources and events that strengthen visual health and inclusion for all.
  • Partner with organisations that share our commitment to evidence-based, meaningful inclusion.

Speaking & Corporate Engagement

Bring Visual Inclusion and Visual Health to Your Organisation

Rose Coloured Lens NPC delivers keynote speaking and corporate engagement sessions that advance visual inclusion South Africa and low vision advocacy. Our sessions are tailored to your audience, industry context, and organisational goals, combining lived experience with evidence-based insights.

We help organisations understand visual health as a core business priority, not a niche concern. Through engaging presentations and interactive workshops, we support leadership teams, HR departments, and diversity and inclusion committees in building credible, sustainable visual inclusion practices.

Our approach is grounded in research, lived experience, and practical application. We work with corporate clients, educational institutions, government bodies, and non-profit organisations across South Africa to create lasting change in how visual health and inclusion are understood and implemented.
