Support Our Work

Your Donation Funds Real Impact

Donations to Rose Coloured Lens NPC go directly to programmes that advance visual impairment awareness, low vision advocacy, and visual inclusion in South Africa. We are accountable and transparent about how every rand is used.

What Your Gift Supports

Your contribution funds keynote speaking and corporate engagement, the development of resources for visual health and inclusion, and community-facing work that builds understanding and access. We do not allocate donor funds to unrelated overhead; we keep our operations lean and mission-focused.

We are a registered nonprofit. Your trust matters to us, and we are happy to answer questions about our work and finances.

To make a donation or discuss partnership, please contact us. We will provide secure payment details and a formal acknowledgment.
