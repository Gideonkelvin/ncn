About Us

A Nonprofit Dedicated to Visual Health and Inclusion

Rose Coloured Lens NPC exists to advance visual impairment awareness and visual inclusion across South Africa. We believe in evidence-based advocacy and the power of visibility.

Our work spans low vision advocacy, corporate visual health strategy, and community engagement so that organisations and individuals can create lasting, meaningful change.

About Founder and Purpose

The Story Behind Rose Coloured Lens

Rose Coloured Lens was founded from a belief that visual health and visual inclusion belong at the heart of how we work and live. Our purpose is to make low vision advocacy and visual impairment awareness visible, credible, and actionable across South Africa and beyond.

We combine lived experience with evidence-based practice to support organisations and communities in building lasting visual inclusion—so that clarity, empathy, and strategy drive real change.

Evidence-Based Advocacy

We ground our messaging in research and lived experience so that low vision advocacy and visual inclusion are understood and actionable.

Corporate Visual Health Strategy

We help organisations build visual inclusion South Africa–ready practices that are credible, sustainable, and aligned with global standards.

Partnership & Trust

We work with partners who share our commitment to visual impairment awareness and meaningful inclusion in every setting.
